Enforcement Framework & Legal Penalties (Housing Act 2004)

Under Section 95 of the Housing Act 2004, failure to comply with any single condition of a selective licence without reasonable excuse is a strict liability offence.

Civil Penalty Notices (Section 249A)

Redbridge Council has statutory power to impose financial penalties of up to £30,000 for each individual breach without court proceedings. Unpaid penalties are enforced via the County Court.

Magistrates' Court Criminal Prosecution

Prosecution results in an unlimited fine, criminal conviction, mandatory notification of mortgage lenders, and immediate revocation of "Fit and Proper" landlord status.

Rent Repayment Orders (RRO)

Tenants or the local authority may apply to the First-tier Tribunal (Property Chamber) for an RRO compelling the landlord to repay up to 12 months' rent collected during the period of non-compliance.

Banning Orders & Rogue Landlord Database

Under the Housing and Planning Act 2016, repeat offenders may receive a Banning Order prohibiting them from letting or managing housing nationwide, plus inclusion on the Rogue Landlord Database.

Redbridge Article 4(1) Direction & Planning Restriction

This Selective Licence applies solely to single-family dwelling occupation (Use Class C3). The London Borough of Redbridge enacted an Article 4(1) Direction on 6 December 2019 that completely removed permitted development rights for small HMOs (Class C4: 3 to 6 unrelated persons).

Converting or letting this property to multiple unrelated sharers requires express planning permission. Doing so without planning consent is both a criminal planning offence and an immediate breach of Licence Condition 1 & Condition 22.

UK Property Licensing Guide for BTL Landlords

Understanding the three tiers of property licensing under the Housing Act 2004 in England:

Part 3

Selective Licensing

Designated by councils in specific wards or entire boroughs to combat poor housing conditions, anti-social behaviour, or low housing demand. Applies to all privately rented properties, even single families or lone tenants.

Part 2

Additional HMO Licensing

Introduced by local authorities for smaller Houses in Multiple Occupation (typically 3 or 4 unrelated tenants forming 2+ households sharing bathroom/kitchen facilities) not covered by mandatory HMO licensing.

Part 2 National

Mandatory HMO Licensing

Nationwide statutory requirement across England for any property occupied by 5 or more persons forming 2 or more households who share basic amenities (kitchen, toilet, bathroom).

Essential Landlord Pre-Tenancy Checklist (National Law):

  • Gas Safety Certificate (CP12): Valid check within last 12 months by Gas Safe engineer. Issued to tenants before occupation.
  • EICR (Electrical Safety): Satisfactory report produced within last 5 years by a qualified electrician.
  • EPC (Energy Performance Certificate): Valid EPC rating of Band E or higher (MEES Regulations).
  • Smoke & Carbon Monoxide Regulations: Working smoke alarm on every habitable floor; carbon monoxide detector in rooms with combustion appliances.
  • Right to Rent Verification: Visual or share-code check verified prior to tenancy start.
  • Tenancy Deposit Prescribed Information: Served within 30 statutory days of receiving the deposit.
  • How to Rent Guide: Current government edition supplied at the start of tenancy.
